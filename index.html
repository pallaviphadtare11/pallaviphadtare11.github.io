Research interests

I am interested in integrating experimental and computational approaches to understand how cellular and molecular changes in neurons and glia contribute to neurodegenerative disease progression. My work in tauopathy and astrocyte biology has motivated me to investigate relationships among glial states, protein aggregation, neuroinflammation, pathology and behavior, while developing computational approaches to integrate molecular, imaging and phenotypic data.
